## Key points

Built at the **Institute of Hydrobiology and Aquatic Ecosystem Management**, [BOKU University](https://boku.ac.at), Vienna. BOKU's cross-domain mandate (forestry, agriculture, water) maps cleanly onto AquaINFRA's marine ↔ freshwater agenda - so the workflow comes from people who **face the data-fragmentation problem themselves**.

## ✅ Key takeaways

- Built by domain experts who live the data-fragmentation problem, not an infrastructure-only team.
- **Specleaner** (`specleanr`) is purpose-built for messy occurrence data.
