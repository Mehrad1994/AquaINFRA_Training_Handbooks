# Research questions

<p class="chapter-meta">Chapter 2 of 3</p>

<div class="chapter-intro">
    <p>The specific environmental objectives driving the HELCOM Baltic Sea use case.</p>
</div>

The Baltic Sea is heavily impacted by human activities and nutrient enrichment. Evaluating the *Effectiveness of Measures* requires bridging the gap between implemented policies and observed marine conditions.

To support this objective, the workflow addresses the following core research questions:

<div class="question-list">
    <ol>
        <li><strong>Eutrophication Reduction:</strong> How effective have existing environmental measures been at reducing nutrient inputs into the Baltic Sea?</li>
        <li><strong>State Assessment:</strong> What is the current environmental state of the Baltic Sea, and how does it correlate with mapped pressures?</li>
        <li><strong>DAPSIM Connectivity:</strong> Can qualitative datasets (Drivers, Impacts, Measures) be successfully harmonised with quantitative data (Activities, Pressures, States) to provide a holistic overview?</li>
    </ol>
</div>
