# The AquaINFRA Project

> [!NOTE]
> **At a Glance**
> - Project goals, consortium partners, and timeframe (2023–2026).
> - Three core components: [AIP]({{ relative_root }}reference/glossary#aip), [VRE]({{ relative_root }}reference/glossary#vre), and [D2KP]({{ relative_root }}reference/glossary#d2kp).
> - Interoperability with EOSC and the Digital Twin of the Ocean (DTO).

The **AquaINFRA project** (January 2023 – December 2026) develops a virtual environment equipped with [FAIR]({{ relative_root }}reference/glossary#fair) data and services for marine and freshwater research.

Our mission is to help scientists restore healthy oceans, seas, and inland waters by bridging isolated research infrastructures and administrative borders.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Official Project Site**: For news and governance updates, visit the [AquaINFRA Project Website](https://aquainfra.eu).

## Core Objectives & Architecture

    <table>

            </tr>
        </thead>

            <tr>
                <td><strong>Marine-Freshwater Integration</strong></td>
                <td>Connects river basin hydrology with coastal oceanography across the land-sea continuum.</td>
            </tr>
            <tr>
                <td><strong>EOSC Interoperability</strong></td>
                <td>Establishes cross-country data discovery powered by EOSC services.</td>
            </tr>
            <tr>
                <td><strong>Spatio-Temporal Analysis</strong></td>
                <td>Offers standardized workflows to model biological, chemical, and physical data.</td>
            </tr>
            <tr>
                <td><strong>Reproducible Science</strong></td>
                <td>Publishes analytical workflows as FAIR Data-to-Knowledge Packages (D2KPs).</td>
