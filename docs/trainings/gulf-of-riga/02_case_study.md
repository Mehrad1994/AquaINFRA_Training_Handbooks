# The Gulf of Riga and Daugava River case study

<p class="chapter-meta">Chapter 2 of 9</p>

<div class="chapter-intro">
    <p>The case study began with divers at the Latvian Institute of Aquatic Ecology noticing the Gulf of Riga growing darker over the years. This chapter turns that observation into a testable question, using decades of <a href="{{ relative_root }}reference/glossary#secchi-depth">Secchi-depth</a> measurements - a simple, long-running proxy for water transparency. Because the gulf is shallow and semi-enclosed, season matters, so the analysis is done per region and per season.</p>
</div>
