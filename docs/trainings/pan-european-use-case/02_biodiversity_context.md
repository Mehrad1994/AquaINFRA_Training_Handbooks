# Introduction to the Research Team

<p class="chapter-meta">Chapter 2 of 6</p>

<div class="chapter-intro">
    <p>Freshwater is the front line for biodiversity loss: under 1% of Earth's surface yet around 10% of known species, and among the most threatened. This chapter explains why bioindicators (fish and macro-invertebrates) are used to read ecosystem health, and introduces the <a href="https://boku.ac.at" target="_blank" rel="noopener">BOKU University</a> researchers facing the data-fragmentation problem.</p>
</div>
