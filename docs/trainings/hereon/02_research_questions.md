# Research questions

<p class="chapter-meta">Chapter 2 of 3</p>

<div class="chapter-intro">
    <p>The core scientific objectives addressed by integrating satellite imagery with in-situ Optical Water Types.</p>
</div>

Evaluating optical water types via satellite presents several technical and scientific challenges. Standard atmospheric corrections or generic algorithms often fail in complex coastal waters where multiple optically active substances (like phytoplankton, suspended sediments, and colored dissolved organic matter) mix.

This workflow is designed to answer the following research questions:

<div class="question-list">
    <ol>
        <li><strong>Algorithm Validation:</strong> How accurately do existing remote-sensing algorithms classify Optical Water Types (OWTs) compared to physical in-situ measurements?</li>
        <li><strong>Spatial Mapping:</strong> Can we generate reliable, continuous maps of different water types across the highly variable conditions of the North Sea and Baltic Sea?</li>
        <li><strong>Data Merging:</strong> What are the best methods for harmonizing high-frequency satellite reflectance data with sporadic, localized marine samples within a virtual research environment like Aqua Galaxy?</li>
    </ol>
</div>
