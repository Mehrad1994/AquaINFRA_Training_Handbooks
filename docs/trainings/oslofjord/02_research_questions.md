# Research questions

<p class="chapter-meta">Chapter 2 of 3</p>

<div class="chapter-intro">
    <p>The core scientific objectives related to storm impacts and long-term marine trends in the Oslofjord.</p>
</div>

Evaluating the environmental impact of storm events requires understanding how a sudden influx of freshwater and land-based materials alters the physical and chemical properties of a coastal sea.

This workflow is designed to answer the following research questions:

<div class="use-case-card">
    <ol>
        <li><strong>Storm Transport:</strong> How do extreme weather events (such as heavy rainfall and storms) influence the transport of suspended sediments and dissolved organic matter from the Glomma River into the Oslofjord?</li>
        <li><strong>Coastal Footprint:</strong> How far into the coastal zone do the effects of these extreme riverine discharges reach, and how long does the marine environment take to recover?</li>
        <li><strong>Long-Term Trends:</strong> Can FerryBox data be used effectively alongside river flux calculations to perform robust, long-term trend analyses of coastal water quality?</li>
    </ol>
</div>
