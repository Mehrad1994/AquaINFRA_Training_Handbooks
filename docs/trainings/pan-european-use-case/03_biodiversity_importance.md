# Why freshwater biodiversity matters

<p class="chapter-meta">Chapter 3 of 10</p>

<div class="chapter-intro">
    <p>Freshwater is the front line for biodiversity loss: under 1% of Earth's surface yet around 10% of known species, and among the most threatened. This chapter explains why bioindicators (fish and macro-invertebrates) are used to read ecosystem health, and why the multi-country Danube - home to the endangered Danube sturgeon and Huchen - is the natural testbed.</p>
</div>
